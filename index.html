FAQ

How do I pay?+

After your order is confirmed in the Telegram or WhatsApp chat, pay by bKash / Nagad (01871690704), Islami Bank transfer, or Binance USDT. There is no payment form on this page — you confirm the exact total with the seller first, then pay with one of the methods in the How to Pay section.

What does “On request” mean?+

It means the product is not sitting in ready stock right now. When you send an order, the seller checks or sources the account for you and confirms in chat whether it can be supplied, at what exact price, before you pay.

Is there a minimum order?+

No. Even a single account is fine — set the quantity to 1 on any product page.

What is the difference between Fresh, Aged and Premium?+

Fresh accounts are newly created and cheapest. Aged accounts are older, which some buyers prefer. Premium is the top-end aged option for that product. The exact age range and details for your order are confirmed in chat.

What do I receive after ordering?+

The login details for the accounts you ordered, plus any recovery information agreed in chat, delivered in the same Telegram or WhatsApp conversation.

Are there any risks I should know about?+

Yes — please read this before ordering. Most platforms, including X, LinkedIn and WeChat, prohibit buying and selling accounts in their terms. A bought account can be locked or banned by the platform, and finance accounts (banks, payment services) are restricted fastest of all. Only order accounts you genuinely need, and ask in chat about the exact product before paying.

Can I order a platform that is not in the catalog?+

Often, yes. Reddit, Discord, ProtonMail, Pinterest and 30+ more are available on request — message @josim06 on Telegram with the platform and quantity.
